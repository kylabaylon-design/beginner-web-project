About El Nido

El Nido is one of the most beautiful destinations in Palawan, Philippines. It is famous for its crystal-clear waters, stunning limestone cliffs, hidden lagoons, and white-sand beaches.

It is a perfect place for travelers who want to enjoy nature, adventure, and peaceful island scenery.

History of El Nido

El Nido was formerly known as Bacuit. During the Spanish period, the area was associated with the settlement of Talindak.

It became an independent municipality in 1916 and was known as Bacuit. On June 17, 1954, Republic Act No. 1140 officially changed the municipality's name from Bacuit to El Nido.

The name "El Nido" means "the nest" in Spanish. It was inspired by the edible nests made by swiftlets, locally known as balinsasayaw, which build their nests in the limestone cliffs of the area.

Today, El Nido is famous for its beautiful limestone cliffs, crystal-clear waters, lagoons, beaches, and rich natural heritage.

Culture & Traditions

El Nido is home to a rich culture influenced by the traditions and way of life of the local communities.

Local traditions include fishing, traditional celebrations, local food, music, and strong respect for nature and the sea.

The people of El Nido value community, hospitality, and protecting the beautiful environment that surrounds them.
